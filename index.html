Our Purpose

Every wheelchair user deserves
more independence — and every
caregiver deserves real peace of mind.

SentryChair bridges the gap between mobility and safety by transforming a standard wheelchair into a connected safety platform — no complicated setup, no internet dependency, just a simple and dependable connection between the chair and the people who matter most.

🧘
Calm by Design
A clean, distraction-free interface that delivers the right information at the right moment.
🔒
Dependably Secure
Local Bluetooth connection keeps your data private and your link to the chair always reliable.
❤️
Built with Care
Designed around real needs — for wheelchair users and the people who support them every day.

Meet the Founder
